1 · Warp tab, HyperWarp line. The top edge is live. Height is how far back you are. The dashed 45° guide means stopped, and steeper than it is reverse. The drawn line goes: flat (normal), step down (repeat), gentle slope (slow), along the guide (freeze), steeper (reverse), then back to the top. The profile shown is Harmo-nicer.

2 · Lock mode, switched on with the 🔒 button next to the dice. Click a section's lock to exclude it from the dice. Amount and Profile are locked by default, and the graph can be locked too. Dice then randomizes everything that is unlocked: the line, Length, Quantize, Skew, the knobs, Output and Mix. Profile shown: Lo-fizzly.

3 · Warp as a modulator. The new Warp slot shows the line's shape as its icon. It is armed (gold), so clicking any dial or the Dry/wet bar routes it, and dragging up or down sets the depth. The gold rings show where it is routed: Stale's Cell size, and Stale's Dry/wet, which is routable now for every modulator. The value follows the line's height at the playhead.
