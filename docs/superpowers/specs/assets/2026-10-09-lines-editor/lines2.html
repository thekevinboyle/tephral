1 · Lines view, a track tab open. The Sequencer gets a Steps | Lines switch. Lines is one big editor, laid out like the Warp tab, with a tab per track: Master first, then one tab per effect in chain order. Each tab shows a mini line, says Line or Steps (whichever plays), and a 🔒 when locked. The graph is the open track's line (white) over the master line (dashed gold). The tinted fill is what you actually get: line × master × the card's Dry/wet. The bar underneath is the warp's: Amount (how deep the line cuts), Length (½ to 16 beats, its own loop), Quantize, Skew.

2 · The Master tab. The master line multiplies every track, Steps tracks included: an open step plays at its Dry/wet × master. A flat master at the top changes nothing. The graph shows every track's line faintly in its colour, so you can see what the master is shaping. The side panel swaps the track controls for an On/Off switch and the multiplier the master is applying right now.
